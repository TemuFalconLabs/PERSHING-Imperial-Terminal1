# DM-only terminal command catalog

Standalone optional entertainment. No game-state, clearance, inventory, puzzle, or combat consequences. Ordinary exact gated commands and accessible record searches take priority. ISB alone navigates only when unlocked. During playback only PAUSE is processed; dark archive remains inactive. Case, spacing, underscore, hyphen and punctuation variations and embedded phrases are recognized; one unambiguous typo on whole nonnumeric inputs of 5–24 characters. No fuzzy authorization. Repeat history is local and resets on reload or new session; unknown replies rotate. Hidden commands omitted from player HELP.
