## Source register and editorial decisions

The [master design](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KbwM-9KeqQCPJ4bx4os9UZB3govfSJyqMgB9w7SJRbs/edit) governs planned reveals and scanner outcomes. The five tabs of the [Chronicle](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1K1hYjktO9KgeKXT7jhCTHZbdyhoPjGXQY0E3rc7rH-s/edit) distinguish completed sessions, proposals, and table rules. The [adult recap collection](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1k5RXsJ4eCeb2b8tvFvvKwHcVlQKQx-SQ) includes Session Six’s recap of Five (Skeld, alarm, Morgan), 5a Broken Spire (asset wording), and Session 2/3/4 intros (Drellis Minor and pursuit). Prior dependency materials and prep extracts supplied candidate callbacks, not evidence that every prepared scene occurred.

The ISB officer’s office contains his partially damaged cylinder and trapped safe; Research Director’s office lies beyond the cloning laboratory and contains her own cylinder and final terminal. All tapes decrypt at ISB. The two physical relays supersede the prototype’s three starters. The unplugged archive terminal replaces loose USB media. The newer code and split govern all props. Equipment and staff incidents are facility-specific fiction. Kids scan outcomes remain deliberately unassigned. Merg and Ben have no approved specimen match determination; the chamber reports it unavailable. The public build uses character callsigns or campaign names only and contains no contact-derived specimen numbers.
